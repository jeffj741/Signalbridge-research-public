Research context

What this instrument can—and cannot—tell us

PressureIQ investigates how temporal context, event trajectories, recurrence, and residual load can reveal distinctions that a single peak or session score might miss. This interactive exhibit is a public demonstration of that research question. The illustrative data here are generated for exploration, not reproduced from the original frozen experiment. Cluster labels and recovery values are descriptive simulation artifacts; they are not validated physiological or clinical classifications.

For scientific review, the original experimental dataset, derivation rules, provenance, and validation evidence must be evaluated separately. This exhibit does not establish treatment efficacy or medical benefit.
