The experience continues forward around the circuit to signal entry with the latest observations and permitted context. A new lap can support better calibration, but it does not automatically authorize stronger intervention. Each response remains bounded by consent, evidence quality, and the governing response rules.

This exhibit is a conceptual, interactive representation of Love Loop's experience architecture. It is not a live physiological measurement, a therapeutic demonstration, or a validated claim of regulation efficacy. Internal thresholds, decision rules, and proprietary implementation details are intentionally excluded.
